# Acme Corp Travel Policy

Version 2026.1 — Effective January 1, 2026. Applies to all employees and
contractors booking business travel through the Acme Travel Assistant.

## 1. Purpose and Scope

This policy governs how employees book flights, hotels, and related travel for
company business. The goal is to balance employee comfort and productivity with
responsible spending. All bookings made through the Acme Travel Assistant are
checked against these rules before they are confirmed. Personal travel is not
covered by this policy and must not be booked through company accounts.

## 2. Flight Booking Rules

### 2.1 Cabin Class

- **Economy** is the default cabin class for all flights.
- **Premium Economy** is permitted for flights with a scheduled duration of
  **6 hours or more**.
- **Business Class** is permitted only for flights with a scheduled duration of
  **8 hours or more**, or for employees at Director level and above on any
  international flight.
- **First Class** is not reimbursable under any circumstances.

### 2.2 Advance Booking

Flights should be booked at least **14 days in advance** whenever possible.
Bookings made fewer than 3 days before departure require manager approval,
because last-minute fares are significantly more expensive.

### 2.3 Price Caps

- Domestic round-trip flights should not exceed **USD 600**.
- International round-trip flights should not exceed **USD 2,500** in economy or
  premium economy, or **USD 5,000** in business class.
- If the cheapest reasonable fare exceeds the cap, the booking requires manager
  approval and a short justification.

### 2.4 Airlines and Routing

- Prefer direct flights when the price difference is under USD 150.
- There is no preferred-airline requirement; choose the best value option.
- Avoid itineraries with layovers longer than 4 hours unless no reasonable
  alternative exists.

## 3. Hotel Booking Rules

### 3.1 Nightly Rate Caps

- Standard cities: up to **USD 200 per night**.
- High-cost cities (New York, London, San Francisco, Tokyo, Singapore, Zurich):
  up to **USD 350 per night**.
- Rates above the cap require manager approval.

### 3.2 Hotel Standards

- Hotels rated **3 to 4 stars** are the standard choice.
- 5-star hotels require manager approval and are only approved when no suitable
  3-4 star option exists near the meeting location.
- Prefer hotels within 5 km of the primary meeting or event location.

### 3.3 Cancellation Policy

- Always prefer **free-cancellation** rates when the price difference is under
  10 percent, because plans change.
- Non-refundable rates may be booked only when they are at least 10 percent
  cheaper than the nearest refundable rate.

## 4. Trip Duration and Alignment

- Hotel check-in should match the arrival date of the inbound flight, and
  check-out should match the departure date of the outbound flight.
- Do not book hotel nights that fall outside the flight travel dates without a
  documented reason (for example, an early arrival for jet-lag recovery on
  long-haul trips, which is allowed for flights of 8 hours or more).

## 5. Approvals and Exceptions

- Any booking that exceeds a price cap, uses a higher cabin class than allowed,
  or falls outside the advance-booking window requires **manager approval**.
- Approval requests must include a one-sentence business justification.
- The Acme Travel Assistant will always show the estimated cost and flag any
  policy exceptions before a booking is confirmed. The final confirmation is
  always made by the employee, not automatically by the assistant.

## 6. Reimbursement

- Submit expense reports within 30 days of trip completion.
- Keep all receipts. Bookings made through the Acme Travel Assistant are logged
  automatically, but employees remain responsible for their expense reports.
- Seat upgrades, lounge access, and in-flight purchases are not reimbursable
  unless pre-approved in writing.
